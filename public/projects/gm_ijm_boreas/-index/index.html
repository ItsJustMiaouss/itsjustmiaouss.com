First released on December 17, 2022 on the Steam Workshop, gm_ijm_boreas is an overhaul of gm_boreas, the iconic scenic winter map originally created by Blueberry_pie.

The original map is an absolute masterpiece, but there were a few specific gameplay elements, interactions, and details I always wanted to tweak for my ow server and roleplay sessions. Instead of keeping those edits to myself, I polished them up and shared them with the Garry’s Mod community!

gm_ijm_boreas, what’s that?#

🚀 Launchable missile#

In the original map, the missile silo was mostly decorative. Every players playing the original map has already tried to launch it. So, I completely reworked the missile system so it can actually be launched! It now features full launch sequences, sound effects, smoke plumes, and a dedicated console command (ent_fire rocket_respawn trigger) to reset the rocket on servers without having to reload the entire map.

🚧 Relocated security checkpoint & electric fences#

To improve roleplay flow and map progression, I moved the security checkpoint further up the mountain road. I also added functional electric fences surrounding the research facility (which server owners can disable via console with ent_remove_all checkpoint_electric_fence) and made sure the security blast doors start closed by default.

⛅ StormFox 2 compatibility & daylight cycle#

One major headache with custom Source engine maps is external weather and day/night cycles. In gm_boreas, the 3D skybox uses a solid radial gradient overlay (skybox_fog.vmt with $nofog) that clashed badly with night lighting. With some advice from Blueberry_pie himself, I adjusted the skybox materials and fog parameters to ensure seamless transitions when running StormFox 2.

🏔️ Terrain & displacement tweaks#

Along with moving props and checkpoints, I resculpted several displacements, smoothed out steep hills, and scattered realistic US road signage across the map using custom models.

The Mapping Pipeline & Custom Tooling#

Working with large Source engine maps in 2024+ is an adventure of its own.

Hammer++ x64#

Compiling a massive map like Boreas with vanilla Hammer is asking for crashes. I switched entirely to Hammer++ on the x64 branch of Garry’s Mod. Surprisingly, a full LDR + HDR compile only takes around 20 minutes on a Ryzen 5 3600X!

Building BSPIntegrator out of frustration#

To pack custom models, sounds, and textures into the final .bsp, people traditionally rely on Vide. However, Vide kept crashing, corrupting files, and failing to detect dependencies on a map of this scale.

Out of frustration, I wrote my own standalone open-source tool: BSPIntegrator. It automated the packing of the embed folder cleanly, without missing assets or corrupting the BSP header.

What did I learn?#

Taking on someone else’s complex Source engine project forced me to level up my mapping and modeling skills fast:

  • Blender & Crowbar: I had to decompile .mdl files, edit collisions and meshes in Blender using Blender Source Tools, and recompile them back to .mdl cleanly.
  • Community feedback balancing: Moving a road checkpoint sounds simple until players point out that custom semi-trucks or Star Wars AT-ATs can’t clear the overhead pipe bridge! Reading through hundreds of comments taught me a ton about how varied server setups and roleplay communities really are.
  • Open source mapping: Sharing the decompiled Hammer sources on Google Drive allowed other server owners and mappers to learn from the project or make their own server-specific tweaks.

🎊 Reception & Numbers#

The map quickly grew far beyond what I initially expected:

  • Over 30,000 subscribers on the Steam Workshop
  • 70,000+ unique visits
  • 4,500+ favorites with a 5-star rating across 1,000+ reviews
  • Included in over 34,000 player collections

Seeing so many servers, machinimas, and community screenshots featuring the revamped base made all the compile hours completely worth it.
